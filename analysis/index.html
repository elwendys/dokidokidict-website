How the simulations work

The simulator walks through each indexed work in sentence order. Counted vocabulary encounters must satisfy both a sighting threshold and a page-spacing threshold. This lets the simulations distinguish the difficulty of entering a work from the difficulty of continuing it after its recurring vocabulary has appeared several times.

Use your own vocabulary in the recommender.

The examples on these pages use synthetic starting vocabularies for comparison. The app can replace them with your reading history, Anki cards, built-in SRS deck, and manually known words.
