From 500 words to 80% of the top 10,000

Three routes through the visual novel staircase.

All three start with the same 500 words. Route 1 is optimized for the easiest next read and vocabulary progress, Route 3 only uses major VNs, and Route 2 is a compromise between the two.

DokiDokiDict recommender analysis · Updated July 25, 2026

The shared rules

  • Start knowing the 500 most frequent words in the Default VN frequency list.
  • Learn a new word after five sightings, with at least five pages between counted sightings.
  • Exclude grammar, punctuation, and proper names; retain the other lexical categories.
  • Allow twenty frequent local words at every measurement point.
  • Respect chronological requirements for sequels and fandiscs.
  • Stop once at least 80% of the top 10,000 Default VN words have graduated.

“Opens” is coverage when that work begins. “30% in” is coverage of its unread remainder after the first 30%. One page is 600 Japanese characters.

This route always takes the highest-comprehension eligible next step over our entire VN corpus. It does not care much about reputation. It spreads the climb over twelve smaller steps.

The shortest route is not automatically the best route

The deep-end route reaches the target in fewer pages than the two gentler routes. That does not mean a beginner should start with CLANNAD because an optimizer said so. Its price is an extremely difficult opening. The smooth route pays for more transitions in exchange for a less severe first wall.

The routes mainly differ in whether they accept one very difficult opening to stay with more highly regarded VNs, or use more works, some of them less highly regarded, to make each transition easier. That is why the app lets you restrict the candidate pool and choose the objective instead of declaring one universal sequence correct.

Important: these are computed examples from a synthetic top-500 starting vocabulary. They demonstrate what sequence planning can do. They are not personal recommendations for every beginner. Use the gallery with your actual vocabulary and remove anything you do not want to read from the candidate pool.
