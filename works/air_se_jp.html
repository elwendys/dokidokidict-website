Japanese reading profile

This indexed edition contains 855,595 Japanese characters across 52,767 sentences. By length it is at approximately the 49th percentile of the 76 indexed visual novels. Its vocabulary breadth is at approximately the 38th percentile within the same group.

Those numbers describe the text, not your personal comprehension. A long work can contain more unique vocabulary while still becoming comfortable through repetition.
