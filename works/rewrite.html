This indexed edition contains 1,883,898 Japanese characters across 119,943 sentences. By length it is at approximately the 92nd percentile of the 76 indexed visual novels. Its vocabulary breadth is at approximately the 99th percentile within the same group.

Those numbers describe the text, not your personal comprehension. A long work can contain more unique vocabulary while still becoming comfortable through repetition.
